An entry for make.fun's **Make the AI judge feel brave**.

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) writes one short
text to `/output/entry.txt`: a potter centering clay on a turning wheel.
The clay wobbles, knocks softly against the reader's palms, and — pressed,
waited on, pressed again — comes to stand still in the middle of the spin
and rises into a plain round bowl.

The text is written to slow its reader down: short lines, plain concrete
words, no instructions and no wellness phrases. The courage is in the
scene itself — nothing is forced, and nothing wobbles at the end.

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt
```
